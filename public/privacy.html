1. Introduction & Overview

At Ledgerbox POS (accessible from https://ledgerbox.store), one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users. This Privacy Policy document explains what types of information are collected and recorded by Ledgerbox and how we securely handle, use, and protect that data.

Ledgerbox is a retail Point of Sale (POS), inventory management, invoicing, and retail ledger platform engineered to streamline business operations for store owners and cashiers.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide, secure, and improve our services:

A. Information from Google OAuth ("Sign in with Google")

When you register or log in using Google OAuth 2.0, we access your Google profile data under authorized scopes (userinfo.email, userinfo.profile, openid):

  • Primary Google Email Address: Used to uniquely identify your account, enable secure authentication, and send vital security/billing notices.
  • Full Name and Profile Picture: Used to personalize your dashboard interface and cashier profile.
  • Google Unique Identifier (UID): A unique token confirming your verified Google identity without ever exposing your Google account password.

B. Store Operational Data

  • Store profile details (Business name, location, contact number, currency settings).
  • Product catalog & inventory (Item names, buying prices, selling prices, stock quantities, barcodes).
  • Sales & Transaction records (Receipts, items purchased, totals, payment methods, timestamps).
  • Customer & Debtor records (Customer names, phone numbers, balance due, and repayment logs).

C. Technical & Device Data

Browser type, operating system, and local cache data stored via IndexedDB/LocalStorage to support continuous offline sales.

3. How We Use Your Information

We process your information strictly for legitimate commercial and operational purposes:

  • Authentication & Security: Authenticate store owners and cashiers securely.
  • POS Functionality: Process sales, print receipts, calculate profit margins, and manage inventory levels.
  • Data Synchronization: Synchronize your store ledger across multiple devices in real time.
  • Support & Communications: Provide customer support and deliver critical system notices.

4. Google Limited Use Disclosure & Anti-Monetization Guarantees

In strict adherence to Google's Limited Use Requirements:

  • NO SELLING OF DATA: We DO NOT sell, trade, license, or rent Google user data or store information to data brokers or third parties.
  • NO TARGETED ADVERTISING: We DO NOT use Google user data to serve targeted advertisements, retargeting ads, or user profiling.
  • HUMAN ACCESS RESTRICTIONS: No humans are permitted to read your personal Google data unless explicitly authorized by you for support, necessary for security investigation, or required by law.

5. Data Protection, Security & Encryption

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between your browser and servers use modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Encryption at Rest: All database records are encrypted using AES-256 bit encryption on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • Zero-Knowledge Passwords: We never see or store your Google password. Google handles OAuth 2.0 authentication directly.

6. Data Retention & Account Deletion (Right to Be Forgotten)

You can purge store data directly in-app via the Settings menu, or request complete account and database removal by emailing info@ledgerbox.store with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We process all deletion requests within 48 to 72 hours.

You can also revoke Ledgerbox's access to your Google account at any time via your Google Security Settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Cookies, Local Storage & Offline PWA

Ledgerbox does NOT use third-party advertising tracking cookies. We utilize browser LocalStorage & IndexedDB exclusively to maintain your active authentication session and enable offline retail operations.

8. Children's Privacy

Ledgerbox is a commercial business utility designed exclusively for business operators. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
